Trulexity Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

Trulexity (“Trulexity,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Trulexity application, website, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

1. Information We Collect

Account and Contact Information

  • Email address
  • User ID or account identifier

We may use this information to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, provide Services, and maintain account-related functionality.

User Content

You may provide content to Trulexity, including:

  • Text prompts and conversations
  • Uploaded documents and files
  • Photos and images
  • Other content that you voluntarily submit

Audio Data

If you use voice-related features, Trulexity may process audio or voice input that you voluntarily provide to enable those features.

Search History

When you use search functionality, we may collect and retain search queries or search history so that search history and related functionality can operate.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate Trulexity Services
  • Authenticate and manage user accounts
  • Process user requests and prompts
  • Provide search functionality
  • Process uploaded images, documents, audio, and other user content
  • Maintain conversation and search history where applicable
  • Improve reliability, security, and performance
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain and improve our Services

3. Third-Party Service Providers

Trulexity may use third-party technology and service providers to operate certain features of the Services, including providers supporting cloud hosting and storage, authentication, artificial intelligence processing, voice and audio processing, search services, and infrastructure services where applicable.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services to Trulexity, subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

4. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain accounts and functionality, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect our Services. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, information about processing, and withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a privacy request, contact Trulexity through the contact information provided on our website.

7. Children's Privacy

Trulexity is not intended for children below the minimum age required by applicable law to independently use the Services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

8. International Data Processing

Depending on the services and infrastructure used by Trulexity and its service providers, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we take appropriate measures to comply with applicable data-protection requirements.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we may update the “Last Updated” date and provide additional notice where required by applicable law.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, please contact Trulexity through the contact information available on our website.
